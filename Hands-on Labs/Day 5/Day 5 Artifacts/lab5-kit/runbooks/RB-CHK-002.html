RB-CHK-002 — Checkout errors or latency high

Owner: team commerce · Dashboard: Shop — Golden Signals (service = checkout)

  1. Open the Golden Signals dashboard for checkout: which signal is red, since when?
  2. Check the dependencies: DB pool of inventory saturated? Payment latency high? (Shop Operations, Saturation row)
