# TEL-D5-503 - instructor notes (AWS CloudWatch)

1. **Per participant:** CloudFormation stack `pager-s3-<seat>` from `../starter/aws/pager-stack.yaml` (Seat parameter). No shared stack is needed. Each stack: 1 Lambda (every minute), 3 custom metrics, 2 alarms, 1 SNS topic (no subscribers), 1 dashboard.
2. **The data pattern** repeats every hour in **UTC**: CPU incident :30-:44 (91 %), one-minute CPU spikes at minutes with `minute % 7 == 3` (93 %), baseline 35-44 %; about 650 requests per minute; error incident :50-:57 (6 %); background errors (3 per minute, about 0.5 %) at minutes with `minute % 5 == 2`. The stack back-fills 6 hours at creation.
3. **Untuned alarms:** `HostHighCPU-<seat>` (Average > 80, 60 s, 1 of 1) has about 7 ALARM episodes per hour; `CheckoutErrors-<seat>` (Sum of Errors > 0, 60 s, 1 of 1) about 11. Learners count them on the graph (+/-2 accepted).
4. **Tuned (solution):** `solution/alarms-tuned.yaml`. CPU: Average > 80, 10 of 10 x 60 s (or 2 of 2 x 300 s), not breaching. Errors: metric math `100*m2/m1` > 5, 3 of 3 x 60 s, not breaching. Expected after tuning: one ALARM per hour each, CPU at about :40, errors at about :52 (UTC). A 5-minute period with 3 of 3 never fires on the error incident: a good discussion point.
5. **Dashboards:** `solution/tier1.json` (4 widgets + text link) and `solution/tier2.json` (16 widgets + text link). Replace `<SEAT>`, `<REGION>`, `<ACCOUNT_ID>` if you paste them into a console.
6. **Quotas and cost:** 40 stacks = 120 custom metrics, 80 alarms (one metric-math alarm costs more than a standard alarm), up to 120 dashboards (3 per seat; check current CloudWatch dashboard pricing and delete them after class), about 58 000 Lambda invocations per day. Check the account limit for dashboards and the Lambda concurrency (1 s runs, no problem).
7. **Clean-up:** delete the participant stacks; delete `ShopNow-Tier1-*` and `ShopNow-Tier2-*` dashboards (created by hand). Custom metrics expire by themselves after 15 months; no action needed.
8. **Fallback without Lambda/IAM rights:** none in this version - the generator needs IAM role creation. Use the instructor account for one shared stack and give learners read access plus rights to create their own dashboards and to *copy* alarms (they edit their own copies of the alarms).
